# Strip N draft | Title

## Concept
- One line: what this strip is about and the feeling it should leave (work back from the ending)
- Role: setup / build-up / turn / climax / afterglow in the whole work; what it picks up from the previous strip and leaves for the next
- Point of view: whose eyes we follow; who appears (ideally at most 3) and where they stand left/right (fixed for the strip)
- Time and place: where, when, weather and light
- Key panel: which panel is the "主役のコマ" (the largest one) and why
- Rhythm: opening (panel 1) → development → turn → landing; which panels are fast, where it slows down, which panel is silent; ends on a punch or on afterglow
- Text budget: how many dialogue balloons and narration boxes
- Options considered: A…; B…; which one and why

## Layout
- N panels: the overall structure in one line
- Panel 1: shape, position (full width / half width), rough share of the page; why
- Panel 2: …
- Reading path: 1 → 2 → 3 … (left to right within a row)
- Structure: the structure code (A–J) the rhythm table gave this strip, and why
- Special devices: 断ち切り / 枠なし / 重ねゴマ / はみ出し / コマまたぎ / 同ポジ / 変形ゴマ, in which panel and for what feeling; "plain grid" if none

## Panel by panel
### Panel 1 | shot size | purpose (establish / action / reaction / emotion / pause)
- Camera: shot size; camera height and angle; distance; focus
- Composition: where the subject sits (off-center); foreground / middle / background; direction of gaze and movement and where the open space is; where the text goes
- People: who is in the frame, where (left/right/front/back), facing where; the frozen moment; action + body part + where they look + prop; expression intensity 0–3
- Background and light: full / simplified / blank / mood background; time and light source
- Text: narration box / dialogue (who) / thought / sound effect: exact words, balloon type, position, reading order, where it is split
- Transition: same moment / next moment / time skip / new scene into the next panel; normal or wider gutter

## Self-check
- While drafting write only "to be checked"; fill it in during the check:
- Checked: checklist items with problems and the verdict, one per line
- Changed: what changed and why, one per line ("Changed: nothing" if nothing)
- Suggestion: anything the user should decide (e.g. too much text, which line could go); omit if none
